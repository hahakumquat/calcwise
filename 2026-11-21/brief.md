Draft: "Rough way to size a severance offer: weekly pay is annual pay divided by 52. Example with made-up numbers: $78,000 a year is $1,500 a week. If the formula is 2 weeks per year of service and you worked 6 years, that is 12 weeks, so $18,000. Unused PTO paid out is separate: $78,000 / 260 working days is $300 a day, so 5 days is $1,500, which makes $19,500 total before tax. At $3,000 a month of expenses that is 6.5 months on paper, but taxes come out of it, so the real number is lower. Check what your own agreement says, since there is no general legal requirement for severance in the US and formulas vary a lot.
