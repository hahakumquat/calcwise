## Reddit (r/Frugal, as an answer in a thread asking if a Black Friday deal is good, not a standalone post)
No image. Re-read the current r/Frugal rules before posting; they limit self-promotion, so the safest route is the math with no link. Add the "I made this" line only if the rules and the thread clearly allow it.

"Quick way to check a sale: compare against what the item usually costs, not the crossed-out price. Example with made-up numbers: 'was' $200, sale $140, so the sign says (200 - 140) / 200 = 30% off. But if you have seen it at $160 recently, the real saving is 160 - 140 = $20, or 20 / 160 = 12.5%. The 'was' price is $40 above what it usually sells for, which is where the extra 17.5 points come from. The catch is you need a price you trust for 'usually', e.g. your own notes or a price-history tool. Disclosure: I made a free calculator that does this math, in case it helps: https://cappycalc.com/finance/black-friday-deal-check/"

Best time: inside a live thread, weekday 8 to 10 am local, Black Friday week.
