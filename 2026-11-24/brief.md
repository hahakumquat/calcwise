Draft: "The math is simple, so you can check it yourself. Annual cost = watts idle x hours idle per day x 365 / 1000 x your rate per kWh. Example with made-up numbers: five devices drawing 15, 10, 1.5, 3 and 2 watts idle is 31.5 W. At 20 idle hours a day that is 31.5 x 20 x 365 / 1000 = 229.95 kWh a year, and at $0.16 per kWh that is about $36.79 a year, with the 15 W one alone at about $17.52. Your real figure depends on the actual watts, which a plug-in power meter will show in a minute, and on your rate (it's on your bill). So the biggest one or two devices are usually where it matters.
