## Reddit (r/Frugal, as an answer in a thread about vampire or standby power, not a standalone post)
No image. Re-read the current r/Frugal rules before posting; they limit self-promotion, so the safest route is the math with no link. Add the "I made this" line only if the rules and the thread clearly allow it.

"The math is simple, so you can check it yourself. Annual cost = watts idle x hours idle per day x 365 / 1000 x your rate per kWh. Example with made-up numbers: five devices drawing 15, 10, 1.5, 3 and 2 watts idle is 31.5 W. At 20 idle hours a day that is 31.5 x 20 x 365 / 1000 = 229.95 kWh a year, and at $0.16 per kWh that is about $36.79 a year, with the 15 W one alone at about $17.52. Your real figure depends on the actual watts, which a plug-in power meter will show in a minute, and on your rate (it's on your bill). So the biggest one or two devices are usually where it matters. Disclosure: I made a free calculator that does this sum for up to 5 devices, in case it helps: https://cappycalc.com/everyday/standby-power-cost/"

Best time: inside a live thread, weekday 8 to 10 am local.
