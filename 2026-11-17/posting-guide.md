## Reddit (r/personalfinance, as an answer in a commute-vs-pay thread, not a standalone post)
Re-read the sub's current self-promotion and disclosure rules before posting. Lead with the math; the link only with the disclosure line.

"One way to compare is a real hourly wage: take-home pay minus work-related costs, divided by every hour the job takes, commute included. Example: $52,000 take-home over 40 h x 50 weeks is $26.00 an hour. A one-hour round trip on 5 days adds 5 hours a week, so the job takes 45 hours (2,250 a year). With $2,500 a year in fuel, parking and lunches: ($52,000 - $2,500) / 2,250 = $22.00 an hour, about 15% lower. Run the same for the other job and compare; pay is only part of the picture, so this is a comparison tool, not a verdict. Disclosure: I made a free calculator that does this math, if useful: https://cappycalc.com/everyday/real-hourly-wage/"

Best time: weekday morning, 8 to 10 am local, inside a live thread.
