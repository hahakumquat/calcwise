## Reddit (r/leasehackr)
No image. Post only as a plain answer in a thread about how excess mileage is projected or what it could cost, not a standalone post. Re-read the current r/leasehackr rules first; I could not check them. If self-promotion or links are restricted, post the math only and drop the final disclosure line.

Draft:
It's just three steps, so you can check your own. Allowance = yearly miles x term in months / 12. Pace = miles driven / months elapsed, projected = pace x term. Fee = (projected - allowance) x per-mile fee in your contract. Example with made-up numbers: 12,000 mi/yr, 36 months, 33,000 driven at month 30, $0.25/mi. Allowance 36,000, pace 1,100/mo, projected 39,600, so 3,600 over and about $900. To land at the allowance you'd average (36,000 - 33,000) / 6 = 500 mi/mo for the last 6 months. It assumes you keep the same pace, your contract has the real fee and any buffer, and some lessors sell extra miles up front, so ask yours. Disclosure: I made a free calculator that does this sum, in case it helps: https://cappycalc.com/finance/lease-mileage-overage/
